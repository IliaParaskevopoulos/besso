How the flow works

Four steps, each built on work elsewhere on this site.

  1. Step 1High-fit lead list

    Leads scored with the ICP fit score out of 300 (corridors, industry, customs owner, live trigger, data access). A-leads score 210 or more.

  2. Step 2Into Claude

    The list goes in with the messaging house and battlecards, so every email starts from the same story.

  3. Step 3Tailored message

    One first email per lead: open with their trigger, lead with the pillar for their role, add peer proof, end with a small ask.

  4. Step 4Sent from besso

    A person reviews and approves each draft; it then goes out from a besso sending address and lands in the CRM.

Guardrails

  • A person approves every email before it is sent. Nothing goes out automatically.
  • Claims come only from the messaging house, with besso's own figures marked as self-reported.
  • Only business contacts who match the ICP, with a clear opt-out in every email.
  • Sending from a warmed-up besso domain, at a pace that protects deliverability.

Fits the stack in the job ad

  • Clay and Sales Navigator build and enrich the lead list.
  • Claude writes the drafts from the messaging house.
  • Lemlist sends the approved sequence; HubSpot logs replies and meetings.
  • The TIM MVP supplies the trigger, so the campaign launches while the topic is live.
