POSITIONING STATEMENT

Global food and drink groups pay duty on billions of euros of goods every year, and the rules that decide how much keep changing. Most rely on their trade team and brokers, with an occasional consultant review that is out of date within months. Besso watches more than 1,250 trade rules and tariff schedules all the time, and its legal research team checks every saving it finds. That is why Danone, Unilever and AB InBev use it to cut duty they can defend in an audit.
