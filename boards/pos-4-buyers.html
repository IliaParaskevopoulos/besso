Who cares most about that value?

THE DECISION

Best fit: global food, drink and consumer-goods groups that move high volumes through many trade lanes and are under cost pressure from new tariffs.

Best-fit profile (target-list ready)ASSUMED

Food, beverage, tobacco or household goods · €5bn+ revenue [ASSUMED threshold] · 20+ import/export corridors · a trade compliance team already in place · a cost-savings programme running · exposure to new tariffs in 2025–26.

Public examplesPROVEN

Danone, Unilever, AB InBev, Coca-Cola, Heineken, Philip Morris International. Second segment: chemicals and agri-science (BASF, Syngenta).

Why they care most

Thin margins on high volumes: a small duty saving per unit becomes a large number. Many lanes mean many rule changes to track.

Not a good fit (for now)

Companies trading in 1–2 corridors (little to monitor) · pure domestic players · services firms · small importers without trade data in an ERP.

WHY

This is the same beachhead as ICP 1 on the value-proposition canvas. Chemicals and agri-science is the next segment. Mid-size exporters stay a growth bet until there is a lighter offer.
