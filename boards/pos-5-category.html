DECISION 5 OF 5

Which category makes the value obvious?

THE DECISION

Choice: a subcategory, 'trade regulation intelligence for duty savings', sitting next to existing compliance tools rather than replacing them.

A · Global trade management software

Buyers assume: workflow, classification, screening, a long IT project. Compared to: ONESOURCE, SAP GTS, Descartes. Hides the value and starts a feature fight against much bigger vendors.

B · Trade regulation intelligence for duty savings ✔

Buyers assume: it finds money and keeps watching. Compared to: consultant duty reviews and brokers, where always-on monitoring wins. Makes the value obvious to the CFO and keeps the IT ask small.

C · New category: 'Trade Cost Intelligence'

Distinctive, but the buyer has to be taught what it is. Too costly for a ~20–50 person company to educate the market.

WHY

Option B is close to the language Besso already uses ('trade regulation intelligence', 'unlock hidden savings'). It frames Besso against the main alternative, where Besso wins, instead of against the software suites, where it would lose on breadth.
