What is the buyer really comparing Besso against?

THE DECISION

Main alternative: the in-house trade team plus customs brokers, with an occasional duty review by a consultancy.

1. In-house team + brokers + periodic consultant reviewASSUMED

Why they pick it: already in place, no new budget, brokers file every entry.

Where it falls short: a review is a snapshot; rules keep changing after it. Nobody watches all lanes all the time.

2. Trade compliance software suitesPROVEN

Thomson Reuters ONESOURCE, Descartes, e2open (Amber Road), SAP GTS.

Why: system of record for classification and origin. Short: big IT projects; built to run compliance, not to hunt savings.

3. Spreadsheets kept by one or two expertsASSUMED

Why: free and flexible. Short: knowledge leaves with the person; cannot keep up with 1,250+ rules.

4. Do nothingASSUMED

Why: duty looks like a fixed cost. Short: the overpayment is invisible, so nobody owns it.

WHY

Besso's customers are Fortune 500 groups that already have trade teams, brokers and often a trade compliance suite. So Besso is rarely replacing nothing; it is competing with 'our people and our broker already handle this'. Ranking is assumed from the customer profile, not from deal data: confirm with besso.
