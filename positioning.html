Positioning phrase

Why we win

  • Always on: 1,250+ trade rules and tariff schedules monitored, against a consultant's one-off review. Proven
  • Legally checked: a legal research team behind each finding, against software-only suites. Proven
  • Peer proof: Danone, Unilever, AB InBev and Heineken already use it. Proven

Where we don't compete

  • The full compliance workflow (screening, filing, export controls): leave it to the suites and sit on top of them.
  • Small importers trading in one or two corridors.

Positioning in one line

besso is the trade regulation intelligence platform for global food and drink groups that keeps finding the duty they overpay across every trade lane, unlike one-off consultant reviews and broker advice.

  1. Main alternativeIn-house trade team plus brokers
  2. Top unique attributeAlways-on monitoring plus legal research
  3. Top valueDuty you stop overpaying
  4. Best-fit buyerGlobal food and drink groups
  5. CategoryTrade regulation intelligence for duty savings

Full statement: global food and drink groups pay duty on billions of euros of goods every year, and the rules that decide how much keep changing. Most rely on their trade team and brokers, with an occasional consultant review that is out of date within months. besso watches more than 1,250 trade rules and tariff schedules all the time, and its legal research team checks every saving it finds. That is why Danone, Unilever and AB InBev use it to cut duty they can defend in an audit.
