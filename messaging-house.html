Core message

besso keeps finding the duty global food and drink groups overpay, and backs every saving with its legal basis.

Taglines: "Duty you no longer overpay." · "Always-on savings, legally checked."

Who we are

Founded in 2023 in Bern, Switzerland. An AI platform plus a trade-law research team, working across about 700 trade agreements. Raised €4.29M in October 2026. [S1, S2]

Brand personality

  • Precise, not pedantic
  • Calm, not alarmist about tariffs
  • Candid, not salesy about savings

Tone of voice

  • Do lead with money and proof. Don't lead with "AI".
  • Do name the rule, the lane, the number. Don't speak in generalities.
  • Do say what besso doesn't do. Don't overpromise on filing or legal advice.
