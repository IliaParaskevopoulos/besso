Legal and regulatory
EU clears signing of trade deal with Indonesia, ending most duties
Agence Europe · 18 Nov 2026
On 18 November the EU Council approved signing the EU–Indonesia CEPA on 21 November. Once fully in place, up to 98.5% of tariff lines will be liberalised, worth about €600 million a year in duty savings. A new agreement with new rules of origin is exactly where importers miss preferences at first, so this looks like a timely opening for Besso with FMCG and chemicals customers that source palm oil and other inputs from Indonesia.
Switzerland and China sign upgraded FTA, relax direct-transport rule
Switzerland Global Enterprise · 30 Oct 2026
The upgraded agreement would make about 77.5% of Swiss exports to China duty-free on entry into force and up to 99.8% in the medium term. Preferential treatment will also no longer always require direct shipping between the two countries. That changes origin eligibility for goods routed through hubs, and is a natural topic for Besso's home-market outreach once both sides finish ratification.
Competitors
Thomson Reuters puts trade, indirect tax and e-invoicing into one AI workflow
Thomson Reuters · 17 Nov 2026
New CoCounsel agentic workflows will give one view of each transaction across indirect tax, Pagero e-invoicing and global trade, starting to roll out in 2027. No FTA or duty features were named. The pitch is breadth across tax and trade, so Besso may want to answer with depth: FTA coverage, explainable origin decisions and savings it can prove.
Customers and partners
Unilever sets €1 billion productivity savings target to end-2029
Unilever via Investegate · 3 Nov 2026
Ahead of its 4 November investor event, Unilever said it will target €1 billion in savings from simplification, process redesign and technology, partly to cover stranded costs from the Foods deal. A named savings programme at a customer is a good moment to show recovered duties as a measurable contribution, and to check how the Foods carve-out changes Besso's scope there.
Product launches
Global Trade Alert opens its trade-policy database to Claude and ChatGPT
Global Trade Alert · 5 Nov 2026
The St. Gallen-backed monitor now has an MCP connector, so general AI assistants can answer trade-policy questions with cited, analyst-checked records. Cited policy tracking is becoming a commodity inside everyday AI tools, which puts more weight on what Besso adds on top: applying rules to a company's own shipment and product data.
