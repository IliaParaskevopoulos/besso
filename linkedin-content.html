Most trade teams overpay on tariffs without knowing it. Not through mistakes. Through lanes nobody has checked against the agreements that cover them. A product can qualify for a preferential rate on one lane and pay the full rate on the next, simply because its rule of origin was never checked. besso screens every product and lane against every agreement, and flags where a lower rate is available, with the legal basis attached. Where would you start: your biggest lane, or the one nobody has looked at? #TradeCompliance #Customs #SupplyChain
