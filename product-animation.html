What besso does, explained in 46 seconds

An animated explainer of the besso platform: the problem, your data in, screening every lane, the legal basis, and monitoring that never stops. Made with AI in one afternoon, with original music. Turn the sound on.

Concept explainer, not official besso material. Lane examples are illustrative.

The storyboard

Six beats, one idea each, built on the messaging house: problem first, then how it works, then proof.

  1. 0:00 · TitleFind the import duty you overpay

    The core message, and the promise: keep finding it as the rules change.

  2. 0:04 · The problemDuty looks like a fixed cost

    ~700 trade agreements and 7,000+ rule changes a week, so nobody owns the overpayment.

  3. 0:11 · Step 1Start from the data you already have

    Shipments, bills of materials and suppliers flow in from an ERP or broker extract.

  4. 0:18 · Step 2Screen every lane

    Each product and lane is checked against each agreement; savings and origin risks light up.

  5. 0:26 · Step 3The legal basis

    A saving on EU milk powder into Indonesia, linked to its rule of origin and reviewed by trade lawyers.

  6. 0:33 · Step 4 and closeKeep watching

    New signals (EU–Indonesia, Switzerland–China, US Section 301), then the close: savings you can defend.

How it was made

Script from the messaging house

Every line comes from the besso messaging house and positioning, so the film says what sales and the website say.

Animated with code

Each scene is built as an HTML animation with Claude, filmed frame by frame and encoded at 1280×720, 30 fps.

Original music

The soundtrack is synthesised for this film, so there are no rights to clear before it goes on a website or LinkedIn.
